### How to Track Hot Flash Patterns Before You Ask for Help

Hot flashes can feel random until you see them on paper. A short tracking window can show whether symptoms cluster around sleep, meals, stress, or no obvious trigger at all.

### Start with the pattern

![Notebook and calendar used for a symptom tracking routine.](~/assets/images/uploads/how-to-track-hot-flash-patterns-before-you-ask-for-help/repo-smoke-canonical-two-inline.png)

For seven to fourteen days, jot down the time, intensity, sleep impact, and what was happening nearby.

### Make the conversation easier

Use the pattern to ask a more specific question about options that fit your health history and goals.
