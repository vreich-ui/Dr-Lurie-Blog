### Build a Menopause Care Plan You Can Actually Use

A care plan works best when it is small enough to follow. Separate what needs evaluation, what needs relief now, and what can be watched over time.

### Sort symptoms into three buckets

![Care-plan notes arranged for symptoms, questions, and next steps.](~/assets/images/uploads/build-a-menopause-care-plan-you-can-actually-use/repo-smoke-canonical-three-inline-1.png)

Put symptoms into urgent changes, quality-of-life symptoms, and background patterns.

### Plan the follow-up

![Phone calendar and checklist prepared for follow-up planning.](~/assets/images/uploads/build-a-menopause-care-plan-you-can-actually-use/repo-smoke-canonical-three-inline-2.png)

A plan should include what to try, what to watch, when to reassess, and what would count as a reason to change course.

### Start with one focused visit

Bring your symptom buckets and the first question you want answered.
