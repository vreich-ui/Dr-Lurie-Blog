### Before Your First Menopause Telehealth Visit

A first visit is easier when the story is already organized. Bring the symptoms that keep repeating, the timing that feels unusual, and the questions you want answered before you are tired or rushed.

### What to have ready

Write down the pattern: sleep disruption, hot flashes, mood shifts, vaginal or urinary symptoms, cycle changes, and anything that has changed your daily choices.

### Next step

If symptoms are affecting sleep, intimacy, work, or confidence, book a focused consultation and bring your short symptom list.
